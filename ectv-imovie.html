Reporters and computer

How we edit stories in iMovie

Keep it short. Keep it true. Show Mr. Macek before you export. No watermarks.

Back to ECTV home Blank scripts in Resources

01 / Prep

Before iMovie

1

Topic approved

Get the topic approved before you film.

2

Three shots

Film wide, close-up evidence, and a clear ending.

3

Sound test

Record a 5-second sound test and listen.

02 / Project

Build on the iPad

1

New movie

Open iMovie and start a Movie project.

2

Clips in order

Add your three clips in story order.

3

Full play

Play the whole thing once with sound on.

Optional AI tools. If your iPad shows AI or enhance tools, use one change at a time. Keep it only if speech is clearer and the picture still looks real. Skip AI if you do not see those buttons.

Trim on the timeline. Keep the story between 15 and 45 seconds.

03 / Cut

Edit by hand

Everyone does these steps.

Trim

Cut waiting, mistakes, and shake.

Length

Stay between 15 and 45 seconds.

Title

One short title with the main fact.

Facts

Remove anything private or unapproved.

Partner

Ask what the story is about.

Teacher

Show Mr. Macek before export.

04 / Finish

Export, upload, share

This is the end of the workflow.

1

Export

Export the finished video from iMovie. Keep the editable project.

2

Upload to school OneDrive

Upload the export to your school OneDrive folder for ECTV.

3

Share with Mr. Macek

Share that OneDrive file with Mr. Macek. Do not publish it yourself.

Don't forget: check in with your homeroom teacher first, always.
