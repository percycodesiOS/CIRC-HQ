01 / Prep

Before iMovie

1

Topic approved

Get the topic approved before you film.

2

Three shots

Film wide, close-up evidence, and a clear ending.

3

Sound test

Record a 5-second sound test and listen.

02 / Project

Build the project

1

New movie

Open iMovie and start a Movie project.

2

Clips in order

Add your three clips in story order.

3

Full play

Play the whole thing once with sound on.

Optional AI tools. If your iPad shows AI or enhance tools, use one change at a time. Keep it only if speech is clearer and the picture still looks real. Skip AI if you do not see those buttons.

03 / Cut

Edit by hand

Everyone does these steps.

TrimCut waiting, mistakes, and shake from each clip.
LengthKeep the video between 15 and 45 seconds.
TitleAdd one short title with the main fact.
FactsCheck facts. Remove anything private or unapproved.
Partner checkAsk a partner what the story is about. Fix anything confusing.
Teacher checkShow Mr. Macek the full video before export.
