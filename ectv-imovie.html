Before you open iMovie

Good filming makes editing easy. Do these four things first.

Don't forget: check in with your homeroom teacher first, always.

1

Get your topic approved

Ask Mr. Macek before you film. That way you never lose work on a story we cannot use.

2

Film three shots

One wide shot to show where you are, one close shot that shows the proof, and one clear ending.

3

Test the sound

Record five seconds, play it back, and listen. If you cannot hear the words, move somewhere quieter and film again.

4

Check what is in frame

No student work, screens, or faces that should not be filmed. When you are not sure, ask first.

Build it with Magic Movie

This is the path we use on the school iPads.

1

Start New Project, then Magic Movie

Open iMovie. Tap Start New Project, then tap Magic Movie.

2

Choose your media

Select the clips and photos for your story, then tap Create Magic Movie.

3

Aim for 30 to 45 seconds

Trim the waiting and the mistakes. Keep one clear title that says the main fact.

Ask for a different length only if Mr. Macek tells you to.

4

Check your facts and your sound

Play the whole thing once. Is every name and number right? Can you hear every word? Fix it now, not later.

Your student path: Start New Project, Magic Movie, choose media, Create Magic Movie.

Export your movie

Exporting makes the video file. Keep the iMovie project too, in case we need a change.

1

Export the finished movie

Use the share or export option in iMovie to make the video file.

2

Choose 1080p if you see it

If iMovie offers a quality choice, pick 1080p. If you do not see that choice, just continue.

3

Turn HDR off if you see it

If iMovie offers HDR, switch it off. HDR can look wrong on the school screens. If you do not see it, just continue.

4

Save it to Photos

Save the export to Photos on the iPad. That is where you will find it in the next step.

Share it and wait for review

Nothing airs until Mr. Macek has watched it. That is the rule, every time.

1

Upload to your school OneDrive

Put the video from Photos into your school OneDrive folder for ECTV.

2

Share it with Mr. Macek

Share that OneDrive file with Mr. Macek by name. Do not post it anywhere else and do not publish it yourself.

3

Tell him it is ready

Sharing a file does not send an alert on its own. Tell Mr. Macek in the ECTV Team or in person that your story is ready.

4

Wait for his review

He watches it and tells you if it is ready or needs a change. Only after that can your story go on ECTV.

A good check before you share: ask a partner to watch it once and tell you what the story was about. If they cannot say it in one sentence, make it clearer.
