How we edit stories in iMovie

For reporters and computer operators. Keep it short. Keep it true. Show Mr. Macek before you export.

Before iMovie

  1. Get the topic approved.
  2. Film three shots: wide, close-up evidence, clear ending.
  3. Record a 5-second sound test and listen.

Build the project

  1. Open iMovie and start a Movie project.
  2. Add your three clips in order.
  3. Play the whole thing once with sound on.
Optional AI tools

If your iPad shows AI or enhance tools, use one change at a time. Keep it only if speech is clearer and the picture still looks real. Skip AI if you do not see those buttons.

Edit by hand (everyone does this)

  1. Trim waiting, mistakes, and shake from each clip.
  2. Keep the video between 15 and 45 seconds.
  3. Add one short title with the main fact.
  4. Check facts. Remove anything private or unapproved.
  5. Ask a partner what the story is about. Fix anything confusing.
  6. Show Mr. Macek the full video before export.

Save

  1. Export to the place Mr. Macek chooses.
  2. Keep the editable project.
  3. Do not publish it yourself.
