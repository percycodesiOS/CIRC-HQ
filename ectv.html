04 / Day

Know the clock

Homeroom release comes first. Always.

8:50

Approved crew arrives

Check in at homeroom. Come only when released. Earlier is better.

8:55

Show starts

Music stops before speech. Producer calls mute and marks. We do not pass lapel mics around.

Kind. Curious. Clear. Good questions. Accurate stories.

Kind

Talk about people the way you want to be talked about.

Curious

Ask useful questions. Check the source before you say it on air.

Clear

Say the point once. Make it easy for a fifth grader to follow.
