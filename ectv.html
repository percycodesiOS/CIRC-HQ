03 / Jobs

Illustrated crew roles

Pick what you want to learn. Bring your best work.

Anchors

Welcome the school. Stay on your mark. Read clearly. Mute before any clip change.

Weather reporter

Check a real forecast. Keep it short and accurate. Use the weather mark, not a walking mic pass.

Camera

One shooting. One watching framing and cables. Camera never wears a show mic.

Computer

Player, graphics, next cue. Keep the board ready. Computer never wears a show mic.

Buddy mornings use pairs when we can: two anchors, two camera, two computer. Weather and producer stay in the booth. Reporters usually work outside the morning booth unless it is their story day.

05 / Day

Know the clock

Homeroom release comes first. Always.

Don't forget: check in with your homeroom teacher first, always.
8:50

Approved crew arrives

Come only when released. Earlier is better when your teacher says yes.

8:55

Show starts

Music stops before speech. Producer calls mute and marks. We do not pass lapel mics around. Move talent to the fixed marks.

Kind. Curious. Clear. Good questions. Accurate stories.
