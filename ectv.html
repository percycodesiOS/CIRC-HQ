How to watch

In classrooms, open School Channel on a school computer the way your teacher shows you. Mr. Macek checks the receiving classroom before we go live.

This page does not start the school broadcast. It just explains the show.

Join the crew

Sign-up is a request. Mr. Macek confirms the approved crew. Check in with your homeroom teacher and get permission before you come to the studio.

Approved crew aims for 8:50. The show starts at 8:55.
