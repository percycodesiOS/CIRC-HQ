The jobs on the crew

Pick the job you want to learn. Every job matters, and every job has one clear thing to get right.

Anchors

You welcome the school and read the news. Stay on your mark, read slowly and clearly, and look at the camera.

Weather reporter

You check a real forecast and tell the school what today feels like. Keep it short, true, and useful for recess.

Camera

You keep the picture steady and framed. Watch the cables and the shot. Camera never wears a show mic.

Computer

You run the clips and the graphics and get the next one ready. Computer never wears a show mic.

On buddy mornings we work in pairs when we can: two anchors, two on camera, two on computer. Weather and producer stay at the desk. Reporters usually work outside the morning booth unless it is their story day.

A sample opening

This is a practice pattern so you can hear the rhythm. It is not today's script.

  1. Good morning, Ehrman Crest. Welcome to the Morning Ehrman Show.
  2. Today we have one school story, the weather, and one clear closing.
  3. Stay kind. Stay curious. Stay clear.

How the morning works

Don't forget: check in with your homeroom teacher first, always.

8:50

Approved crew arrives

Come only on the day you were released. Earlier is better once your teacher says yes.

8:55

The show starts

Music stops before anyone speaks. The producer calls the mutes and the marks. We do not pass mics around, we move to the marks.

Kind. Curious. Clear. Good questions and accurate stories.
